# Vision

## Why Foodpilot exists

Many restaurants rely on software solutions that are expensive, inflexible, or limit access to their own customer and operational data.

Foodpilot aims to provide a modern, open-source platform that gives restaurants full control over their ordering, delivery, and business operations.

## Goals

- Standalone ordering interface
- Simple integration into existing restaurant websites
- Modern user experience
- Restaurant management
- Order management
- Delivery management
- Customer management
- Statistics and reporting
- Open API
- Extensible architecture
- Full ownership of business and customer data

## Non Goals

- Restaurant marketplace
- Restaurant discovery platform
- Social network
- Food delivery network
- Full restaurant website builder
- Content management system

Foodpilot focuses on ordering, checkout and delivery operations.

Restaurants can integrate the ordering flow into an existing website through a simple link or call-to-action button.
